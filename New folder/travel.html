★ 4.9Monsoon

India ⌄

Ponmudi

A winding road into the clouds and Kerala's quiet green highlands.

Hover me to know the route

From ₹2,200 / person2 days minimum

Couple package from ₹4,000

  • Golden Valley
  • Peppara Wildlife Sanctuary
  • Meenmutty Falls
  • Deer Park
  • Kallar River

A refreshing hill escape known for its hairpin bends, misty viewpoints, and vivid green tea and spice country. Slow down, breathe in the mountain air, and explore the forest trails.

October to March; monsoon brings dramatic green landscapes.

Carry a light rain jacket, start the drive early, and check forest access before setting out.

★ 4.9Winter

India ⌄

Munnar

Endless tea gardens, cool air, and the kind of quiet that stays with you.

Hover me to know the route

From ₹3,200 / person3 days minimum

Couple package from ₹5,900

  • Eravikulam National Park
  • Mattupetty Dam
  • Top Station
  • Tea Museum
  • Attukad Waterfalls

Set high in the Western Ghats, Munnar pairs rolling tea estates with misty peaks and cool mornings. Wander through fragrant plantations or take a scenic drive to the region's panoramic viewpoints.

September to March for crisp weather and clear mountain views.

Pack a warm layer for evenings and book popular park visits ahead of time.

★ 4.8Winter

India ⌄

Goa

Golden beaches, Portuguese lanes, and evenings made for lingering.

Hover me to know the route

From ₹3,800 / person4 days minimum

Couple package from ₹7,000

  • Palolem Beach
  • Fort Aguada
  • Basilica of Bom Jesus
  • Chapora Fort
  • Dudhsagar Falls

Goa is more than a beach break: discover colorful old quarters, lively markets, quiet coves, and a delicious blend of coastal cuisines.

November to February for sunny, comfortable beach days.

Rent transport from a licensed provider, dress respectfully at heritage sites, and protect the coastline by leaving no litter.

★ 4.8Summer

India ⌄

Manali

Himalayan trails, riverside cafes, and crisp mountain air.

Hover me to know the route

From ₹3,500 / person4 days minimum

Couple package from ₹6,500

  • Solang Valley
  • Hadimba Temple
  • Old Manali
  • Rohtang Pass
  • Jogini Falls

Manali blends cedar forests and Himalayan scenery with a lively mountain-town spirit. Spend a day exploring the old town, then head out to a valley or a nearby trail.

March to June for pleasant weather; December to February for snow.

Mountain roads can change with weather. Check local conditions and carry layers, even in summer.

★ 4.7Winter

India ⌄

Jaipur

Rose-colored palaces, lively bazaars, and royal Rajasthani heritage.

Hover me to know the route

From ₹2,800 / person3 days minimum

Couple package from ₹5,100

  • Amber Fort
  • Hawa Mahal
  • City Palace
  • Jantar Mantar
  • Jal Mahal

Jaipur's pink facades, grand forts, and artisan bazaars make every wander feel like a discovery. Explore the old city on foot and leave time for a sunset view over the Aravalli hills.

October to March, when daytime temperatures are comfortable.

Start sightseeing early, carry water, and agree on a fare before taking local transport.

★ 4.7Summer

India ⌄

Darjeeling

Tea-scented mornings, toy train rides, and Kanchenjunga views.

Hover me to know the route

From ₹3,000 / person3 days minimum

Couple package from ₹5,500

  • Tiger Hill
  • Batasia Loop
  • Darjeeling Himalayan Railway
  • Peace Pagoda
  • Happy Valley Tea Estate

Darjeeling's mountain light, historic railway, and tea gardens reward travelers who take their time. Wake early for a Himalayan sunrise and explore the hillside lanes at an easy pace.

March to May and October to November for clearer skies.

Sunrise viewpoints get busy; bring warm clothing and check train schedules before your visit.

★ 4.8Winter

India ⌄

Udaipur

Lakeside sunsets, marble palaces, and romantic old-city streets.

Hover me to know the route

From ₹3,100 / person3 days minimum

Couple package from ₹5,700

  • City Palace
  • Lake Pichola
  • Jag Mandir
  • Saheliyon Ki Bari
  • Monsoon Palace

Udaipur's lakes and palaces create a graceful backdrop for a relaxed city break. Take a boat across Lake Pichola, then spend the evening exploring the old town's small galleries and cafes.

September to March for cooler weather and pleasant evenings.

Reserve popular lake activities in advance and dress modestly when visiting temples.

India ⌄

Rishikesh

River breezes, forested foothills, and a restorative riverside rhythm.

Hover me to know the route

From ₹2,400 / person2 days minimum

Couple package from ₹4,400

  • Lakshman Jhula
  • Triveni Ghat

★ 4.7Monsoon

India ⌄

Alappuzha

Drift through palm-fringed backwaters and Kerala's village life.

Hover me to know the route

From ₹2,600 / person2 days minimum

Couple package from ₹4,800

  • Alappuzha Beach
  • Vembanad Lake
  • Kuttanad
  • Marari Beach
  • Pathiramanal Island

Alappuzha is a front-row seat to Kerala's backwater life: quiet canals, green paddy fields, and coastal sunsets. Explore by boat with an operator committed to responsible, low-impact travel.

November to February for drier days; monsoon for lush scenery.

Confirm boat inclusions and timings in advance, and bring sun protection for the water.

★ 4.6Winter

India ⌄

Hampi

A surreal landscape of ancient temples and giant boulder-strewn hills.

Hover me to know the route

From ₹2,300 / person2 days minimum

Couple package from ₹4,200

  • Virupaksha Temple
  • Vittala Temple
  • Stone Chariot
  • Matanga Hill
  • Lotus Mahal

Hampi's immense boulders and remarkable Vijayanagara-era ruins create an open-air history experience unlike anywhere else. Explore the site thoughtfully and take time to enjoy the landscape between monuments.

October to February, when days are cooler and more comfortable.

Wear sturdy shoes, bring water, and follow posted guidance around protected monuments.

★ 4.5Summer

India ⌄

Shimla

Colonial-era charm, pine-scented walks, and sweeping Himalayan views.

Hover me to know the route

From ₹2,900 / person3 days minimum

Couple package from ₹5,300

  • The Ridge
  • Jakhoo Temple
  • Kalka–Shimla Railway
  • Christ Church
  • Kufri

Shimla's tree-lined promenades, mountain air, and historic rail journey make it a classic Himalayan retreat. Explore the pedestrian center, then venture out to nearby forest and valley viewpoints.

March to June for mild days; December to February for winter scenery.

The town center is best explored on foot. Reserve the heritage train early during peak season.

★ 4.8Summer

India ⌄

Andaman Islands

Clear blue water, quiet islands, and coral-rich adventures.

Hover me to know the route

From ₹5,200 / person5 days minimum

Couple package from ₹9,600

  • Radhanagar Beach
  • Cellular Jail
  • Elephant Beach
  • Neil Island
  • Baratang Island

The Andaman Islands pair brilliant water and forested shores with rich marine life. Split your time between island beaches, heritage in Port Blair, and a responsible reef experience.

October to May for calmer seas and better conditions for island hopping.

Check ferry schedules, follow marine-park rules, and never touch or remove coral.
